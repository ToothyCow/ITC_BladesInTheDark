Thieves and spies try to survive in a haunted City full of powerful selfish people 

The characters want to know more and they want revenge. 
- What are you willing to pay to uncover the truth and seize control of your fortunes? 
- What cost is too high to pay?
- What will you do when the bill comes due? 

The Hive says that soiling themselves with dark dealings with spirits is a step too far. No riches are worth the cost of your soul
The unseen says that altruism exposes themselves, and that secrecy must be kept above all else 
The reconciled say that complacency is death, that accepting your fate is weakness, and that hurting the weak is not inherently evil if it is required to survive 
The Lord emperor says that to cede control would be deeply irresponsible and lead to far more chaos, pain, and death than he has ever caused. Anything that threatens his rule must be crushed, no matter the cost. Better for his subjects to submit to a monster and live than to rebel and die
